Applying for work.

What do I need before I start?

Your contact details, work preferences, a food handler’s card and three professional references. For each reference, have their name, company, email, phone and how they know you. A résumé, TABC certificate and cover letter can also be included. See the application guide.

Can I go back and change an answer?

Yes. Use Back or a completed step to review your answers. Your entries stay on the page while you move between steps. They are not saved after you leave or refresh.

Which files can I upload?

The food handler’s card and TABC certificate accept PDF, JPG or PNG. Résumés and cover letters accept PDF, DOC or DOCX. Each file can be up to 10 MB, with 18 MB in total.

Are the skills questions translated?

The original role-specific skills questions are currently in English. The surrounding website and application controls are available in English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. Contact admin@workwithkeww.com if you need help before applying.

What if I cannot confirm my submission?

Keep the reference shown on the page and contact admin@workwithkeww.com. If the page says a manual check is needed, do not send a second application until the team has checked it.

Staffing & software.

What should I include in a staffing inquiry?

The location, dates, roles and approximate headcount, along with your venue and contact details. The team needs to confirm coverage and availability. Send an inquiry.

Does requesting a demo or trial create an account?

No. These are requests for a conversation with KEWW. They do not book staff, provision an account or activate a trial.

How can I ask about pricing or coverage?

Describe your requirements in the inquiry form. Pricing and service availability need to be confirmed for your location and needs; there is no public price list here.

Your account.

Where do existing users log in?

Open the KEWW app. App access is separate from this website’s worker application and business inquiry.

How do I contact KEWW?

For general questions or help using this website, email admin@workwithkeww.com. For staffing or software requirements, use the inquiry form. No response time is promised on this page.
