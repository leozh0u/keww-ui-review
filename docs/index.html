Questions.

Are the watches available now?

Watch tools are in development. The workflow shown here describes the plan, not a released watch product. Request a demo to discuss current tools and your team’s needs.

What is contingent staffing?

Contingent staffing means bringing in workers as needed for temporary assignments, events, or changes in demand, rather than filling every role with a permanent employee.

How do I apply for hospitality work?

Complete the KEWW application with your personal details, work preferences, certifications, references, and relevant experience. Have your certification documents ready before you start.

Can I apply for part-time work?

Use the application to share the roles and work preferences you are interested in. Available shifts and schedules depend on current opportunities.

Is staffing available in my area?

Staffing and work availability vary by location. Send a staffing inquiry to confirm coverage for your venue, or check the current application for the roles you are interested in.
