BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Before you get started.

Find the right setup for your team, your events, and the way you work.

How do staffing, team software and add-ons fit together?

You can approach KEWW for contingent staff or buy workforce software for your own team. Industry add-ons cover specialised workflows: Kasheww is the catering add-on, currently in pilot.

Can we use our own crew?

KEWW supports planning around your own team, with the option to request additional hospitality workers when you need coverage. A demo can help you identify the right setup.

Are the watches available?

Watch tools are in development. The device render and workflow shown are conceptual, not released integrations.

How does the event-day tablet help managers?

The illustrated tablet view brings task statuses, worker assignments, and issues together. Ask for a demo of the current workflow and availability.
