Before you get started.

Can we use our own crew?

The product direction supports teams managing their own roster as well as contingent staffing. Ask about the pilot and the workflow your business needs.

Are the watches available?

Watch tools are in development. The device render and workflow on this page are a concept, not a demonstration of a released integration.

Are these live analytics?

No. The example illustrates how a task could become a useful operational record. Available reports and data connections must be confirmed for your pilot.

How do we get started?

Request a demo to discuss your catering or event workflow. A demo request starts a conversation; it does not create a software account or confirm a staffing booking.
