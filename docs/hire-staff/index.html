Before you book.

What is contingent staffing?

Contingent staffing means bringing in workers for temporary assignments, events or changes in demand. It lets a business discuss staffing around a specific need rather than only permanent roles.

Can KEWW staff my location?

Include your location in the inquiry so the team can confirm coverage. This website does not promise availability in every region.

Where can I see pricing?

Include the roles, dates, location and headcount in your inquiry. Pricing needs to be confirmed for your requirements; there is no published price list here.

Can I ask about the software only?

Yes. Choose a software demo in the inquiry form and describe what you’d like to see. A demo request does not provision an account.
