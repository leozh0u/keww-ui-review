Roles and shifts depend on current opportunities and location. An application does not guarantee work or a particular schedule.

Before you apply.

The application covers your details, work preferences, certifications, a role-specific skills quiz and three professional references. You’ll review everything before sending it.

  • Have your food handler’s card ready to upload.
  • Bring contact details for three professional references: name, company, email, phone and how they know you.
  • Keep your résumé and any TABC certification handy if you want to include them.
  • You can add a cover letter or upload one. Both are optional.

Files can be PDF, Word documents or images, depending on the field. The form shows accepted types and limits. Your progress stays on the page while you move between steps; it isn’t saved after you leave or refresh.

Good to know.

Can I apply for part-time work?

Yes. Share your availability and preferred working times in the application. Available work depends on current opportunities.

Can I choose more than one role?

Yes. Select the roles you’re interested in. Your skills questions follow that selection.

Do I need to create an app account first?

The website application is available separately from the app login. Existing account holders can log in here.

What if positions won’t load?

Use Retry in the application. If the list still isn’t available, contact hello@workwithkeww.com. The form won’t send an application with an unconfirmed role.
