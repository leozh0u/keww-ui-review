Catering, in one workspace.

Kasheww is the catering add-on for KEWW, bringing proposals, menus, event orders and client communication into an event workspace.

Request a demoLog in↗

Currently in pilot. General availability, pricing and rollout need confirmation with KEWW.

Keep the event together.

Catering work moves between a client’s request, the agreed menu and the instructions the service team uses. Kasheww brings those parts into the same event workflow.

Proposals & menus
Prepare the offer and menu around the event’s requirements.
Event orders
Keep service details in the banquet event order, or BEO, for team review.
Client communication
Keep questions and requested changes connected to the right event.
Invoices & records
Work with the event’s documents and history, with review before sharing.

A catering pilot, with a clear scope.

Kasheww is being piloted for Cafe Natalie. The focus is the catering event workflow; adoption and the tools it can replace need to be agreed with the business.

Existing document, CRM and accounting tools should remain in use until their replacement is accepted. A pilot does not promise a complete migration or a native watch integration.

Kasheww questions.

Is Kasheww generally available?

Kasheww is currently in pilot. Ask KEWW about suitability, access and rollout for your business.

Do we need to hire KEWW staff?

No. KEWW supports software for an existing team, contingent staffing and industry add-ons as distinct routes. The available package and terms need confirmation.

Will it replace all our current tools?

That depends on the workflow and what your business accepts. Discuss your current documents, CRM and accounting process before planning a migration.

Show us how you run an event.

Bring your current proposal, BEO or event workflow to the conversation. We can discuss the pilot’s scope and fit.
